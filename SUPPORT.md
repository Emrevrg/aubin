## English

When I started this project I had no team and no budget: just one computer, free GPU quotas and a big goal. I'm a 17-year-old high school student, and I kept building AUBIN through financial hardship, even paying for the AI tools I use with difficulty. Every training run meant waiting for the weekly free quotas to reset.

Even so, AUBIN has measured results today:
- **#1** on the typed-decisions benchmark, ahead of Laya, meraGPT and Jev.
- Ahead of Jev on **all 8 metrics** of Jev's own published split.
- **69.3%** on ScreenSpot for visual computer use, and an FPS agent that learns while it plays.

Every number comes with code and result files, so anyone can reproduce it.

My goal is to grow **Norovox** into a strong, open AI venture from Türkiye that anyone can use. For that I need support and investment.
- **Where support goes:** GPU compute, model training, and the AI development tools this work depends on (such as Claude). I build AUBIN with them every day, and paying for them is one of the hardest parts.
- **What supporters get:** a place in our acknowledgments and early access to new releases.

To support, invest, or simply talk, reach me at **zgremre@gmail.com** or **emrevrgdev@gmail.com**.

Bu projeye başladığımda elimde ne bir ekip ne bir bütçe vardı; sadece bir bilgisayar, ücretsiz GPU kotaları ve büyük bir hedef vardı. 17 yaşında bir lise öğrencisiyim. AUBIN'i maddi zorluklar içinde, kullandığım yapay zekâ araçlarının üyeliklerini bile zorlukla ödeyerek geliştirmeye devam ettim.
